**Issue #4; November 25, 2026 (PST).** PR #116's frozen four-input comparison was negative: all four exact-turn final linear averages missed the `0.01` initial-pot absolute gate, and wet A regressed slightly against its matched q8 control. Its published report already contains complete exact-row best-response contribution vectors. This distinct, read-only question asks whether the remaining losses and paired changes are concentrated in a few turn or river decisions. The answer may direct a later prospective strength experiment; it cannot establish that changing any one action improves play.

Exact row index is the common alignment between paired vectors. A source, identity or arithmetic mismatch is a correctness stop, with its partial evidence retained.

## Frozen metrics and thresholds

For each game and street, report candidate and control positive contribution totals in chips and initial-pot fractions; top-one and top-ten **candidate residual** shares of candidate street total; positive candidate-minus-control row-difference mass, negative offset, net change and top-one/top-ten positive-worsening shares. Also report top-ten improvement share of negative offset, and the share carried by PR #116's 32 previously selected diagnostic rows. Rank absolute candidate contributions descending, positive paired differences descending and improvements ascending; break every tie by exact row index ascending. Retain complete vectors and fixed top-ten indices, including zero rows. Never substitute a more favorable checkpoint, arm, street or row. A zero denominator is reported as `none`, not zero percent.

Call a street residual **concentrated** when its top ten candidate rows carry at least 50% of its positive total; otherwise **distributed**. Apply the same 50% rule separately to positive paired worsening and negative paired improvement; use `none` if the corresponding mass is zero. These are descriptive diagnostic labels, not poker-strength acceptance. Preserve the original PR #116 absolute gate of candidate exploitability `<=0.01` initial pots (and redundant worst `<=0.025`), whole coverage `>=0.95`, and zero recall violations. Preserve its paired rule: at least three final differences `<=-0.001` pots, including dry and wet, with the fourth `<=+0.001`; any difference `>+0.001` or absolute/coverage/recall failure is negative. Recompute these only as identity checks against the published **negative** PR #116 decision, never as a new classification or default promotion. The new result is a distribution description, regardless of its direction.

Best-response row gains depend on each arm's saved opponent policy and optimized reply continuation. A paired row difference is additive accounting, not a causal one-action swap or universal advice. The report must distinguish known policy outcome from this new data-only ranking and must not infer a confidence interval from deterministic, related games.
