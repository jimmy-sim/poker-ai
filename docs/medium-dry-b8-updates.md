## Definition of done and question

The [saved-policy diagnosis](river-generalization-diagnosis-report.md) found no information collision on the finite medium/dry exact-river candidate, yet 75.51% of its remaining mean best-response gain came from river decisions, and its current policy was much weaker than its averages. This suggests unfinished or unstable learning, without proving its cause. The [B1 matched-work test](medium-dry-b1-updates-result.md) used noisier one-sample updates and worsened all three medium/dry paired seeds. It does not establish that lower-variance updates help.

**One distinct question:** does averaging **eight** IID external samples before each signed-regret update (B8), instead of published **four** (B4), reduce medium/dry turn/river exploitability at the **same charged node work** without degrading known low/wet? B8 has fewer policy updates at fixed work but lower variance in each batch mean. This tests the opposite direction from B1. It is one candidate, not a B1 retry or batch-size sweep; an unfavorable result does not authorize B16.

Done means source correctness and exact qualification, a fictional proof, fixed compute profile and separate admission, at most one admitted own run, own-before-reference publication, complete paired metrics and decision readouts, final checks and an honest positive, negative or inconclusive report. A failed source, compute, numerical or scientific gate stops this experiment with partial evidence retained.
