river representation and matched **charged tree-node work**, not equal wall
time. Lower exploitability means a smaller gain available to an optimal
opponent within this restricted game.

## Frozen decision

linear-average exploitability **0.025118160532 initial pots**, above its
absolute 0.01 gate, and is worse than the B4 seed median by
**+0.007973007818 initial pots** (positive means worse). The three paired
candidate-minus-control differences are −0.006169020327,
+0.009811425285 and +0.007973007818, in seed order 20261511–13. The new
low/wet in-position candidate is `7.298588510e-8` initial pots: its absolute
gate passes and its +`6.272548527e-8` median difference is inside the fixed
+0.001 nondegradation bound. The medium/dry absolute failure and paired
regression independently trigger the frozen negative rule. All four
medium/dry paths fail the 0.01 absolute gate; all low/wet paths pass. No
checkpoint, seed, case, representation or threshold was changed after seeing
these values. No default is promoted.

## Representative saved decisions

The [raw report](../results/river-full-broader/report/report.json) retains the
control-ranked top three additive best-response gain nodes per street and
their same-node readouts for every arm and checkpoint. On the medium/dry turn
`As Jc 5d 3h`, seat 0 with `9h Jh` can fold, call, raise to 144, or raise to
182. At final linear average, median B4 seed 20261513 chooses
`[0.961304, 0.038683, 0.000010, 0.000003]`; the full candidate chooses
`[0.977468, 0.022282, 0.000214, 0.000036]`. Their additive best-reply
gain contributions at this node are 0.332544 and 0.034703 chips, respectively.
The full candidate is better at this **particular** control-ranked node even
though its whole-game medium/dry result is worse. A representative river node
on `As Jc 5d 3h Qd` with the same private cards has fold, call, raise to 146:
the median B4 saves `[0.494596, 0.503042, 0.002363]`, while full saves
`[0.492069, 0.507236, 0.000695]`.

These values assume each saved opponent policy and an optimal reply
continuation. Counterfactual reach, saved-prefix reach, all alternative-action
values and exact cards are in `diagnostics` in the raw report. A local node
score is not the whole exploitability contribution for a street and is not
universal poker advice. Turn and river gains in the table sum to the finite
game's exact exploitability in chips; the candidate's medium/dry remaining
turn gain (0.861096 chips) is much larger than its river gain (0.043158).
