# Prospective matched-work test of the unchanged exact-turn learner

**Issue #4; November 24, 2026 (PST).** This protocol is to be published before any new 20m/40m policy, profile outcome, or rank. [PR #113](known-turn-loss-attribution-result.md) ended **inconclusively** at a saved-path binding guard; its wet partial and missing dry candidate remain failed evidence. This study does not correct or retry that one-shot diagnostic. It asks a different poker-strength question using the **unchanged** PR #108/110 representations and learner: does additional matched charged work reduce the substantial residual weakness on the already known wet turn-start game, and is any gain specific to the finer turn keys rather than merely more compute?

## Why this question, and what is fixed

The [known wet result](known-wet-turn-mechanism-result.md) found that exact acting-turn information reduced final linear-average exploitability from 0.061847 to 0.029805 of the initial pot at 10,117,956 charged visits, but still failed the frozen 0.01 absolute target. Its [saved-policy diagnosis](known-wet-residual-diagnosis-result.md) found concentrated turn best-response gain and diffuse river gain, with no acting turn/river key collisions. The final exact-turn **current** policy was 3.298 chips versus 1.073 chips for its linear average; the average had fallen from 31.246 chips at the 1m checkpoint. These observations are consistent with incomplete learning but do not prove further work helps. [PR #112](known-turn-regret-recency-result.md) tried a different signed-regret discount and made this wet game worse while helping the dry game. Testing a **fixed longer work budget with the original learner** is a separate hypothesis. Here a best response means the strongest strategy against a fixed saved opponent in the finite loaded game; NashConv/2 is half the sum of the two seats' unilateral improvement, in chips, so smaller is better.

The [machine contract](../configs/known-turn-work-budget.json) fixes exactly two previously outcome-inspected games: `collision_medium_wet_oop-02` (`Qs Kd As 3c`, known wet primary, five weighted private combos per seat) and `full_new_medium_dry_oop` (known dry contrast). Their exact paths and SHA-256 hashes are pinned. Neither is held out or unseen, and the dry case is not a substitute selected after a wet result. Control is `signed_full_q8_turn_exact_river`; candidate is `signed_full_exact_turn_river`. Keep the same game, chance, seats, payoff signs, actions, 36-chip initial pot, private range weights and exact river keys within each pair. Only the acting turn information grouping differs. The original signed full expected traversal, regret update, linear average, exact uniform initial current/average policies and target order seat 0 then seat 1 stay unchanged. No warm start from published 10m policies: those archives do not contain the complete regret state. A single continuous new path per arm/game crosses the 10m checkpoint on its way to 40m; do not launch a separate 10m replay or pick a favorable path.

Use PCG64 seed **0** for each of four paths. This full expected traversal consumes no random draws, so changing nominal seeds would reproduce the same learning trajectory and would **not** create uncertainty samples. Stop each path at the first complete target-pair iteration crossing **10m, 20m and 40m charged node visits**, counting initialization and zero-reach nodes as in PR #110. Save actual work/overshoot and both current and linear-average policies at every checkpoint. Match actual charged work between arms within a game; reject a mismatch. The 10m crossing is a prospective reproducibility check against the eight pinned old current/average policy hashes and the two old actual-work counts, not a chance to select or discard outcomes. A mismatch is a correctness stop. Retain all four paths, all checkpoints and failures.

## Frozen metrics and conclusion

Primary lower-is-better metric: exact restricted-game NashConv/2 divided by the loaded game's **36-chip initial pot**, at final **40m linear average**. Report all four policies at all three work checkpoints and both readouts (24 cases), with candidate-minus-control paired values at equal actual work for **each** known game. Also report acting-seat turn and river additive best-response gains, complete row vectors, zero-recall checks, whole and post-initial-river positive-average uniform-reach coverage, table bytes, worker and launcher runtime, peak RSS, hardware, source revision, lock hash and dirty state. Fixed previously inspected exact rows are wet `1611, 4041, 7506, 1679, 1406` and dry `2445`; retain zero-reach entries. Rank final candidate top ten turn/river contributions by additive gain, ties by exact row index, and show the same selected rows for both arms and all checkpoints. Give board/private cards, legal concrete actions, saved probabilities, chance-times-opponent counterfactual and full saved-prefix reaches, optimized-continuation alternative-action values and contributions. The two arms have different saved opponents, reaches and continuations; local values are neither a causal action-swap estimate nor universal poker advice.

Call this **positive on the two known games only** if at 40m both candidate final averages are at most **0.01 initial pots**, their worst is at most **0.025**, both candidate-minus-matched-control differences are at most **−0.001 pots**, both whole coverages are at least **0.95**, and both recall counts are zero. Otherwise call it **negative** if all required scientific and correctness gates pass; a failed gate or incomplete four-path result is **inconclusive** for poker strength. Report the control's absolute gates separately. Descriptively, call the wet candidate's budget trend `continuing decline` only if its 40m minus 10m score is at most **−0.005 pots** and its 40m minus 20m score is at most **−0.001**; otherwise call it `not established`. This trend is not strength acceptance or proof of eventual convergence. The 10m values may reproduce historical values, but any mismatch is retained and investigated as correctness, never used to select a seed. No statistical confidence interval or broader transfer claim follows from two related, deterministic, already inspected games. No default promotion follows even from a positive known-game result.
