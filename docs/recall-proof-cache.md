## Fixed question and acceptance

Can a source-bound experimental adapter reuse an already successful predicate
result when exactly the original checker's structural inputs stay unchanged?
Keep its function/file, all original arithmetic, archived inputs, policy/book
bytes, seeds, work labels and every other guard unchanged. This is a read-only
comparison of existing OWN cost archive0, not new learning or an admission run.

The fingerprint includes validator version and all field names, dtypes, shapes
and C-order bytes for children, valid, player, information and EVERY levels
array. Those are the inputs consumed by the pinned original checker. Policy,
payoff and chance probabilities are not cached; their original computations and
checks still run. No policy, reply action or reference output is a cache value.
Unsupported array types/object dtypes fall back to the original checker. Only
successful proofs for stable before/after fingerprints may enter the cache.
A changed field must miss and revalidate; failed checks never become successes.
Assume a single-threaded caller owns stable tree data during a check, as the
original reader does; do not claim concurrent-mutation safety.

Tests cover successful reuse, every structural field and level mutation,
unequal-depth/forgotten-own-action failures, mutated-back structures, rejected
checks not cached, unsupported arrays, unchanged nonstructural data, and mutation
during validation. A tiny complete replay compares actual computed summaries
and full stored receipts under original versus cached hooks. No check is simply
deleted. Geometry hashes are recomputed on EVERY request.

   mutations. The certificate includes the validator version, field names,

   summaries and policy/book bytes on this complete saved archive, supported by
   structural mutation and rejection tests. It would not prove concurrent-mutation
