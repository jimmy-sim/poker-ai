## Definition of done and scope

Compare unchanged `signed_full_q8_turn_exact_river` control with unchanged `signed_full_exact_turn_river` candidate at matched charged work on **four newly drawn medium, out-of-position turn-start games**: two dry and two wet, in fixed order dry A, wet A, dry B, wet B. The public betting template is the previously used `configs/learning-spots/dev_medium_oop.json`; its pot is 36 chips, stacks 182 each and action abstraction/prefix unchanged. Only board and private-card ranges are new. Both arms share each exact game, chance, payoff signs, legal actions and river keys; the candidate only splits acting-turn q8 groups into existing exact acting keys. The original full expected signed regret update, exact uniform initialization, target order 0 then 1, linear regret and average weights remain unchanged. PCG64(0) is recorded but unused: **one deterministic path per arm/game, no seed uncertainty**. No default promotion or full-game claim.

Selection deliberately **conditions on at least four collided exact acting-turn rows per input**. A collision is distinct exact acting-turn observations sharing one q8 strategy key. This makes the test informative about the diagnosed grouping weakness, but conclusions apply **only to collision-containing medium OOP inputs selected by these rules**. They do not estimate collision prevalence, unseen public templates, all boards/ranges, or poker play outside the restricted games. The previous PR #107 zero-collision cohort and PR #109 failed three-stratum screen remain separate, with their failures intact; its two selected medium inputs are now previously inspected and excluded. The low/wet stratum is outside this new question after its prior one-time structural exhaustion; this does not revise or rescue the old three-stratum result.

Done requires: published prospective protocol; isolated selection/training adapters and correctness tests; exact-source guarded base and optional Python 3.11/3.12 local checks, all-file hooks and both hosted jobs; one score-free one-time structural screen and frozen cohort; a fictional own proof; one complete fixed profile and separately published strict compute admission; at most one admitted eight-path study; all own policies/scores published before pinned comparison-only reference audit; fixed report and final exact-source local/optional/hosted checks; solo review and history-preserving merge if eligible. Count exact initialization, all terminal and zero-reach nodes and actual overshoot; paired actual work must match within each spot at every checkpoint. No warm start or selected checkpoint.
