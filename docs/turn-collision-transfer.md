# Prospective collision-conditioned exact-turn transfer test

**Issue #4; November 23, 2026 (PST).** This plan and its [machine contract](../configs/turn-collision-transfer.json) must be committed, pushed and verified before drawing or structurally screening any new board/range candidate. [PR #107](turn-exact-transfer-result.md) stopped before training because its three newly drawn two-combo inputs had zero turn-key collisions. The [PR #108 known-game test](known-turn-exact-mechanism-result.md) then improved one already studied medium/dry game with 12 collided exact turn rows at matched work. The open question is whether this unchanged representation improves decisions on **new physical card/range combinations that actually contain the intended grouping opportunity**. This is a conditional finite-game mechanism test, not an unseen public template, random population estimate or default-promotion gate. Prior negative, inconclusive and failed results remain unchanged.

## Definition of done and fixed question

Compare the unchanged `q8_turn_exact_river` control with the unchanged exact-acting-turn/exact-river adapter under the same full expected signed learner and charged work on three prospective, structurally selected inputs. Done means one published one-time structural screen and frozen cohort; isolated, tested source without touching historical pins; required exact-source base/optional Python 3.11/3.12, hooks and hosted checks; one fictional proof, fixed profile and separate compute admission; at most one admitted own comparison; own publication before the pinned comparison-only audit; fixed report and final exact-source checks, review and history-preserving merge. A failed correctness, publication, numerical, compute or selection gate is retained and ends this experiment. An unfavorable poker result is classified once; no changed seed, spot, representation, cap or threshold to obtain acceptance.

## One-time spot selection before outcomes

Use a single `PCG64(20261624)` stream and fixed stratum order: (1) medium/dry out-of-position, (2) medium/wet out-of-position, (3) low/wet in-position. The first two use the pinned `dev_medium_oop` public betting template; the third uses the pinned `heldout_low_oop` template, whose name does **not** make it unseen. The public action menu, pot, stacks and betting prefix come from the template; add one legal check only when needed to make the declared seat act next. For each candidate, draw an ordered four-card turn board without replacement, require four distinct ranks and the previously published dry/wet texture definitions, and exclude the `(sorted flop ranks, turn rank)` signature of **all 24 pinned prior spots** across seven directories, regardless of suits. Exclude every earlier screened candidate's signature too. Allow at most **10,000 board draws** for each candidate; exhaustion stops the experiment with evidence.

For each eligible board, draw **20 distinct remaining physical cards** to make five private combos per seat and draw ten independent integer range weights in `{1,2,3}`. Set actor/hero to the declared position and `max_tree_nodes=200000`. Validate syntax and build the exact game, original q8-turn/exact-river partition and unchanged exact-turn adapter. A syntax error, oversized tree, guard failure or **900-second whole selection cap** stops and retains the partial screen; no replacement. Compute structural turn collision counts only: an exact acting turn row is collided if its original q8 key also contains a distinct exact acting turn row. Record the raw candidate spot, SHA-256, board signature, attempt number, exact/q8 row counts, collided turn rows/groups, river key identity, tree nodes, runtime and any failure for **every** screened candidate. Do not compute policy scores, best responses, reference outputs or training outcomes during selection.

Screen at most **20 valid board/range candidates per stratum**, in fixed RNG order. Freeze the **first** with at least **four** collided exact acting turn rows; never rank eligible candidates by collision magnitude or poker outcome. If any stratum has no qualifying candidate by its twentieth, publish the complete partial screen and stop before training as **inconclusive for strength**. The cohort is deliberately conditional on opportunity; its performance cannot estimate collision prevalence or improvement over general boards. Publish and GitHub-byte-verify the tested selector source before this one-time screen, then publish and verify the full screen/freeze before any policy path.

## Paired learning, metrics and classification

For each frozen input in stratum order, run q8 control then exact-turn candidate from exact uniform initialization, both `PCG64(0)`, target order zero then one. Full expected traversal does not consume RNG, so this is **one deterministic path per arm**, not multiple independent seeds. Six paths total; three fixed workers receive path index modulo three. Keep exact game/chance/payoffs/owners/actions and all river keys identical. The only representation change is exact acting turn information. Use unchanged signed regret, linear regret and average weights. Charge every visited tree node, including zero-reach nodes, and exact uniform initialization. Save current and linear-average policies at the first complete target-pair crossing **100k, 1m and 10m** charged node work; retain actual work and overshoot. No warm start or post-outcome tuning.

Primary lower-is-better outcome: exact restricted-game NashConv/2 at final linear average, in chips and fractions of the **initial pot**. Report every same-spot candidate-minus-control pair at all three checkpoints, with no seed confidence interval or population inference. Also report whole and post-initial-river positive-average uniform-reach coverage, recall violations, turn collision inventory, exact and abstract information rows, table bytes, runtime, hardware and peak RSS. Rank the control final policy's top three additive best-response gain rows on each of turn and river, plus top three collided turn rows, ties by exact row index; display the **same rows** for both arms at all checkpoints. Show board, private cards, legal actions, saved probabilities, counterfactual and saved-prefix reach, alternative-action values and additive contributions. These values assume the saved opponent policy and optimized reply continuation, and are not universal poker advice or causal estimates of one action change.

Historical candidate absolute gates apply **separately to each of the three inputs**: final exploitability ≤**0.01 initial pots** and worst ≤**0.025** (each spot has one deterministic candidate), whole coverage ≥**0.95**, recall violations **zero**. Report control gates too. **Positive** requires all three candidates pass absolute gates, at least two final paired differences ≤**−0.001 initial pots**, and the third ≤**+0.001**. **Negative** means any candidate absolute gate fails or any paired difference >**+0.001**. Other complete outcomes are **inconclusive**. This does not promote a default or establish full-game play.

## Compute and publication boundaries

Planning forecast only: the PR #108 two-path 10m run took 14.516 launcher seconds on the known two-combo input; its slowest fixed 1m-repeat scaled to **42.768 seconds per 10m path**. Five combos per seat create up to `25/4=6.25` times as many private-card pairings as two each, though tree and scoring cost need not scale that way. A simple six-path planning heuristic is `6 × 6.25 × 42.768 ≈ 1,604` path-seconds, or about 535 seconds across three balanced workers; it is **not** compute admission. The prior 12-policy native audit took 12.902 launcher seconds; 36 policies with larger trees may take materially longer.
