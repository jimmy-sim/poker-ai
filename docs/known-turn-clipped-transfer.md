This is one distinct learning-rule question, not a retry of that candidate.

## Why this question

The earlier [controlled-learning grid](../results/controlled-learning/report/checkpoints.csv) measured an already known exact-information, full expected medium game at 10m charged work: clipped/linear/linear regrets and averaging scored **0.050212445%** of the initial pot versus **0.289021880%** for signed/linear/linear. That finite result suggests a representation and traversal interaction worth testing. It is counterevidence that [PR #100's clipped B4/q8 test](medium-dry-clipped-regret-result.md) worsened all six paired seeds on two other known games. PR #120's signed/uniform/linear change worsened all four games below. Neither observation selects a favorable seed, input or checkpoint here.

The question is whether **clipping accumulated negative regret to zero** instead of retaining it improves the unchanged exact-turn/full expected learner's 40m decisions on **all four** PR #116 known medium OOP games at matched work. Clipping changes the learner's regret ledger; it does not change its acting-turn or river information, public action menu, cards, ranges, payoff, initialization, target order, chance treatment or strategy averaging. This study will not attribute any difference specifically to representation versus traversal, because those conditions differ from PR #100. The four games were selected, trained and inspected in PR #116 and revisited in PR #118–120. They are development cases, neither unseen nor independent samples.
